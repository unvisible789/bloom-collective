# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in Bloom Collective a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and inclusive
- Being patient with others who are learning
- Giving constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing when we make mistakes
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

Project maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, and also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.
